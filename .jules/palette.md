## 2024-05-23 - [Add ARIA labels and focus states to Chatbot icon-only buttons]
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like Chatbot toggles and Send buttons) often lack accessible names, making them invisible or confusing to screen readers. Furthermore, standard interactive elements sometimes miss explicit, visible focus states needed for keyboard navigation. Adding dynamic `aria-label`s and consistent `focus-visible` ring utilities ensures these crucial interface controls remain fully accessible and usable for all users.
**Action:** Always verify that every icon-only button contains a clear, descriptive `aria-label` (updating dynamically if state-dependent) and incorporates standard focus styling (e.g., `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-4 focus-visible:ring-primary focus-visible:ring-offset-2`) to guarantee both screen reader compatibility and robust keyboard accessibility.
