## 2024-05-24 - Add Accessibility to Floating Action Buttons
**Learning:** Floating action buttons (like Chatbots) that use icon-only toggles often miss essential `aria-label` and `aria-expanded` attributes, making them opaque to screen readers despite their prominent visual position.
**Action:** Always ensure icon-only floating action buttons include descriptive `aria-label` properties, `aria-expanded` state tracking, and clear visible focus rings (`focus-visible`) to maintain accessibility across interactive widgets.
