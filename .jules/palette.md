## 2025-04-10 - Added Aria Labels to Icon-Only Chatbot Buttons
**Learning:** Found that the chatbot in this specific app relies heavily on icon-only buttons for toggling visibility and sending messages (`MessageCircle`, `X`, and `Send` icons from `lucide-react`), which are completely invisible to screen readers without an explicit text alternative. Additionally, managing the expanded/collapsed state dynamically through `aria-expanded` and an alternating `aria-label` provides crucial context.
**Action:** When implementing similar floating chat interfaces or modal toggles in this design system, always provide dynamic `aria-label`s reflecting the action (e.g., "Open chat" vs "Close chat"), accompany toggle actions with an `aria-expanded` attribute tied to the boolean state, and ensure send buttons within small forms have descriptive labels.
